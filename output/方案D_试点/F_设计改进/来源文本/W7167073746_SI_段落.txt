[段落 13] Experimental Section
[段落 14] Preparation of KB/S and KB/S-AXT cathodes composites: The sulfur-carbon composite materials were prepared using a conventional melt-diffusion method by mixing Ketjen black (KB, ECP600JD, purchased from Canrd) and sulfur (S, 99.99%, purchased from Aladdin) with mass ratio of 3:7. Subsequently, the mixture was heated to 155 ℃ for 12 h to ensure sulfur melted into the carbon substrate. The composite cathodes were fabricated by the high-energy ball-milling method. The synthesized KB/S was mixed with Li6PS5Cl (LPSC, purchased from Shanghai Yili New Energy Technology Co., Ltd.) electrolyte in a weight ratio of 4:6 and loaded into sealed ZrO2 jars. The composite cathode materials were obtained by 500 rpm ball-milling for 6 h. The KB/S-AXT cathode materials were obtained by adding 0.5–10 wt% astaxanthin (AXT, 96%, purchased from Macklin).
[段落 15] ASSLSB assembly: All processes were performed in a glove box with H2O and O2 < 0.1 ppm. First, 100 mg of LPSC electrolyte powders were cold-pressed at 200 MPa into a polytetrafluoroethylene (PEEK) sleeve (diameter of 10 mm) to form the solid-state electrolyte layer. Then, the prepared composite cathode powders were pressed on the one side of the SSE pellet under the same pressure for 3 min (mass loading: 1–1.5 mg cm–2). As for high-sulfur-loading batteries, 25-35 mg of composite cathode powder were added to the positive electrode side to control the sulfur loading to approximately 6-10 mg. Finally, the Li metal or Li-In alloy anode (purchased from Tianqi Lithium Corporation) was cut into disc with a diameter of 10 mm and attached to another side of the electrolyte layer under the pressure of 100 MPa. The additional pressure of 50 MPa was provided through the stainless steel frame during cycling. As for symmetrical battery, both positive and negative electrodes used 10 mg cathode composites.
[段落 16] Electrochemical measurements: All assembled cells were rested for 4 h at room temperature prior to testing. The galvanostatic charge/discharge processes of ASSLSBs were conducted using the Neware battery test system under 30 ℃ in the voltage range of 1.5–3 V (vs. Li/Li+) or 0.8–2.5 V (vs. Li+/Li-In). Nyquist diagrams were recorded on the CHI770E electrochemical workstation (CH Instruments) at the frequency range of 10 MHz to 0.1 Hz with an amplitude of 10 mV. CV curves were collected in the same voltage range as charge/discharge processes by CHI770E electrochemical workstation. For operando EIS measurements, the cells were operated on the electrochemical workstation under a scanning rate of 0.03 mV s–1 and the corresponding data was recorded within the same frequency range from 0.1 Hz to 10 MHz. All the DRT analysis were calculated using the accessible MATLAB program DRTtools created by the research team of Professor Francesco Ciucci. The galvanostatic intermittent titration technique (GITT) measurements were conducted under 30 ℃ using a Neware battery testing system. After activation for two cycles at the current density of 0.05C, the GITT tests were performed within a voltage range of 1.5–3 V (vs. Li/Li+) by applying a constant current pulse at 0.1 C for 10 min, followed by a rest period of 30 min under open-circuit conditions.
[段落 17] Material characterizations: 31P solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy data were collected with a Bruker Avance Neo 400WB spectrometer. The crystal structures of powders were characterized by X-ray diffraction (XRD, Bruker D8 ADVANCE) with Cu Kα radiation (40 kV, 100 mA, λ = 1.54056 Å). The chemical states of the materials were analyzed using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS, PHI 5000 VP III) with monochromatic Al Kα radiation (10 mA, 15 kV). A special vacuum sampling device is adopted to avoid exposure to air. Time of flight secondary ion mass spectrometry (ToF-SIMS) spectra were conducted using an ION-TOF TOF-SIMS 5 machine in the negative-ion mode using a 30-keV Bi3+ cluster primary ion gun with a measurement area of 50 × 50 µm2 and a 1 keV 60 nA Cs+ ion beam for depth profiling with a sputter area of 200 × 200 µm2. The sputter rate was set to ~0.84 nm s-1 on GaN.
[段落 18] The operando pressure monitoring of ASSLSBs: The operando pressure monitoring of ASSLSBs with different cathodes was conducted with a tailor-made apparatus. The force sensor was rated for 100 kg (SBT732-100kg, Simbatouch) and paired with the corresponding signal conditioner. The force sensor was installed at the top of the battery mould, which allows to monitor the homologous stress response during discharge/charge process. The KB/S|LPSC|Li and KB/S-AXT|LPSC|Li cells with sulfur loading of 3.6 mg cm–2 were cycled at 0.05 C at 25 ℃ within a voltage range of 1.5–3 V (vs. Li/Li+). All pressure variations during discharge/charge process were recorded using custom software.
[段落 19] Computational details: All first-principles calculations were performed within the Materials Studio platform. Geometry optimizations and electronic structure analyses were carried out using the DMol3 module with the Perdew-Burke-Ernzerhof (PBE) functional under the generalized gradient approximation (GGA) framework, combined with the DFT-D2 correction to account for dispersion interactions. A double numerical plus polarization (DNP) basis set and DFT semicore pseudopotentials (DSSP) were used. The convergence thresholds were set to 1 × 10–5 Ha for energy, 0.02 eV Å–1 for forces, and 0.005 Å for atomic displacements.
[段落 20] Adsorption energies (Eads) were calculated using Equation (1), more negative values indicate stronger interactions. Partial density of states (PDOS), differential charge density, and electrostatic potential (ESP) analyses were conducted to probe orbital hybridization, charge redistribution, and polarization channels. Bader charge analysis was employed to quantify charge transfer.

[段落 23] Transition states were located using the linear synchronous transformation/quadratic synchronous transit‌ (LST/QST) method in DMol3 and further verified with the climbing-image nudged elastic band (CI-NEB) method in CASTEP, using 5–7 intermediate images and a maximum force criterion of 0.03 eV Å–1. Mayer bond order analysis was performed to evaluate the relative strength of P–S and Li–Cl bonds across initial, transition, and final states.
[段落 24] Interlayer expansion upon lithiation was modeled with Forcite molecular dynamics simulations using the COMPASS II force field at 300 K in the NVT ensemble. Successive Li+ insertion steps were carried out under fixed in-plane lattice constants to obtain the relative expansion ratio ΔV/V0, allowing a direct comparison between Gh and Gh-AXT systems.
